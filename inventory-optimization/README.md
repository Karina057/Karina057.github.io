# Inventory Optimization Analysis

**Type:** Self-directed Supply Chain practice project  
**Tools:** Excel, inventory management concepts

## Goal
Analyze a small synthetic sales dataset and estimate inventory planning measures that can support replenishment decisions.

## What I did
- Reviewed 12 months of unit sales.
- Calculated annual and average monthly demand.
- Estimated Economic Order Quantity (EOQ).
- Estimated safety stock using a service factor of 1.65.
- Estimated a reorder point using a 14-day lead time.

## Key results
- Annual demand: **6,420 units**
- Average monthly demand: **535.0 units**
- Estimated EOQ: **453 units/order**
- Estimated safety stock: **79 units**
- Estimated reorder point: **325 units**

## Takeaway
The analysis shows how demand, ordering cost, holding cost, and lead time can be combined to create a simple replenishment plan.

> **Note:** The dataset is synthetic and was created for portfolio practice; it does not represent a real company.
